9. In reason, provide concise visible evidence only. Do not provide hidden reasoning or chain-of-thought.

Every box count must be a non-negative integer or null. Negative values and decimals are invalid. Read row_number from a visible row number; when no row numbers are printed, number product rows from top to bottom starting at 1.
